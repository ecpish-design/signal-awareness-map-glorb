## Interpretation notes used in GLORB

- ACARA Personal and Social Capability provides the educational basis for emotional awareness, reflective practice and emotional regulation.
- AERO is used as an evidence-informed school reference for recognising changing regulation needs, responding early and supporting recovery/return.
- ABS rating-scale guidance supports clearly labelled ordered response options and treating “don’t know” separately. ABS also notes that rating-scale responses are ordinal; GLORB therefore treats its percentages as descriptive summaries rather than precise standardised measurements.
- W3C guidance informs the one-clear-step-at-a-time interface, visible labels, consistent controls, large touch targets and avoidance of nested scrolling.
- NCTSN is used only to support the concept that everyday cues can remind some children of prior experiences. A response in GLORB does not imply trauma.
- The Gomez et al. review is used cautiously to support considering sensory/environmental input as one possible influence on regulation, with substantial individual variation.
- The Mazefsky et al. review is used to explain why custom self-report summaries must not be represented as standardised validated results.
